# **App Name**: GreenLoop

## Core Features:

- AI Waste Detection: Utilize AI image recognition tool to identify and categorize recyclable waste (PET, HDPE, cardboard, metals, e-waste) from user-submitted photos or live camera feeds, providing an estimated points value.
- Waste Preparation Checklist: Guide users through a mandatory checklist of steps (e.g., cleaning, sorting) to ensure waste meets quality requirements before submission.
- Pickup Scheduling: Allow users to select a convenient date, time slot (with eco-bonuses), and confirm their location for waste collection.
- Reward Points & Redemption: Track earned points from successful waste pickups, display the user's current balance, and enable redemption of available vouchers and rewards.
- Personal Environmental Impact Dashboard: Display a personalized dashboard showing total points, recycled weight, CO2 emissions avoided, and other relevant environmental metrics.
- Pickup Confirmation QR Code: Generate a unique QR code for each scheduled pickup, serving as verification for drivers upon collection of recyclables.
- Social Impact Sharing: Enable users to share their recycling achievements and environmental contributions on social media platforms like LINE, Facebook, and X (Twitter).

## Style Guidelines:

- The application adopts a light color scheme, reflecting a fresh, clean, and eco-friendly aesthetic.
- Primary color: A vibrant, balanced green (#38C24C) to represent growth and environmental stewardship, used for calls to action and key data.
- Background color: A subtle, desaturated green (#EBF5ED) provides a calming and clean canvas for content.
- Accent color: An energetic orange-yellow (#F7BB0E) highlights rewards, bonus points, and important alerts, ensuring good contrast and visual pop.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for its modern, slightly technical, and legible character in titles and important numerical displays.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for clean, objective readability in longer descriptions and general UI text.
- Utilize simple, clear, and modern emoji-style icons (e.g., 🧴, 📦, 🎁) to convey waste types, actions, and rewards quickly and universally.
- Employ a mobile-first, card-based layout with clear sectional divisions, prioritizing readability and ease of navigation on smaller screens. A fixed bottom navigation provides persistent access to core app functions.
- Incorporate subtle micro-animations for feedback, such as toast notifications, progress bar fills, and smooth screen transitions, to enhance the user experience without distraction.
